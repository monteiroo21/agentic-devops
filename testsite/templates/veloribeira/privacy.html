Privacy policy

Velo Ribeira, Lda. collects only the information it needs to handle your rentals, tours and orders: your name, your contact details, an ID number for rental deposits and your payment details, which are processed by our payment provider and never stored by us.

We keep rental and order records for as long as the law requires (currently ten years for invoices) and delete everything else after two years of inactivity.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@veloribeira.pt.
