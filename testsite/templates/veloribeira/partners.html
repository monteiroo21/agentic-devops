Partners and friends

We work with a handful of local businesses and groups who share our love of riding in the north of Portugal. If you're planning a longer trip, these are the people we'd call.

Tours and holidays

Douro Ride

Family-run bike tours in the Douro Valley, based in Peso da Régua. Our customers get 10% off with the code VELORIBEIRA. douroride.pt · Bike and wine tour

Bikes and gear

Trailgear Supply

Our main supplier of racks, bags and wheels for touring and gravel bikes. Bought a Trailgear part from us? Register it for the 5-year warranty. trailgear-supply.com

Routes

We publish our favourite local routes on PedalMap, so you can load them on your phone or GPS:

  • Porto to Matosinhos seaside loop (24 km, flat)
  • Gaia riverside and Serra do Pilar climb (18 km, one steep climb)
  • Parque da Cidade family loop (9 km, no traffic)

Community

Ciclovia Norte

A volunteer group campaigning for safe cycle lanes across Greater Porto. They run a free bike-maintenance clinic on the first Saturday of every month. ciclovia-norte.pt

Porto Bike Fest

The city's cycling festival, with a family ride along the river and a gravel race in Gaia. We run the rental and repair tent every year. portobikefest.pt
