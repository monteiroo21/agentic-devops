About Velo Ribeira

We opened in 2014 in an old cork warehouse by the river, with twelve rental bikes and a workbench. Today we're a team of nine: mechanics, guides and people who simply like riding around Porto.

We rent around 80 bikes, run guided tours from March to November and sell a small range of city, touring and gravel bikes that we'd happily ride ourselves.

Come and say hello: the shop is open every day from 9:00 to 19:00.
