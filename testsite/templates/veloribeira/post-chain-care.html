Chain care basics: clean, lube, repeat

A clean, well-lubricated chain shifts better, runs quieter and lasts twice as long. Porto's sea air and wet winters are hard on chains, so here's the routine our mechanics use.

Clean

Every few weeks, or after any ride in the rain, wipe the chain with a rag while you turn the pedals backwards. If it's black and gritty, use a chain-cleaning tool or a brush with a little degreaser, then rinse and let it dry completely.

Lube

Put one drop of lube on each roller, turn the pedals for a minute, then wipe off the excess. Lube belongs inside the chain, not on the outside, where it only picks up dirt. Use wet lube in winter and dry lube in summer.

Check for wear

Chains stretch as they wear, and a stretched chain wears out your cassette and chainrings too. A chain checker costs a few euros; replace the chain when it reads 0.5% on an 11- or 12-speed bike, or 0.75% on anything older.
