Coastal ride to Vila do Conde

Full day · 52 km · easy · €65 per person

We leave Ribeira at 9:30 and follow the river to Foz, then turn north along the seafront through Matosinhos, past the fishing harbour and the long beaches of Leça and Angeiras. Lunch is grilled fish in Vila do Conde, near the old aqueduct, followed by a short visit to the shipyard museum. We take the metro back to Porto with the bikes and arrive around 17:30.

Included

  • Hybrid bike (or an e-bike for €15 more), helmet and lock
  • Guide, snacks and water
  • Metro ticket back to Porto
