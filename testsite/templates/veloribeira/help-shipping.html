Shipping & delivery

We deliver accessories and parts anywhere in mainland Portugal in 2–3 working days for €4.90, free on orders over €60. Deliveries to Madeira and the Azores take 5–7 working days.

Bikes

We deliver bikes fully assembled within Greater Porto for €25, or you can collect yours ready to ride from the shop. We don't ship bikes outside Portugal.

Click and collect

Order online and collect from the shop on Rua da Fonte Taurina, usually on the same day.
