Warranty & repairs

Every bike we sell is covered by the manufacturer's warranty: lifetime on most frames and two years on components. Bring the bike and your receipt to the shop and we'll handle the claim with the manufacturer for you.

Free first service

New bikes get a free check-up within three months of purchase, once the cables have stretched and the bolts have settled.

Repairs

Our workshop repairs any make of bike. Most jobs are done the same day, and we'll call you with a quote before starting anything over €30.
