Returns & refunds

Changed your mind? You can return anything bought online within 14 days of delivery, and anything bought in the shop within 30 days, as long as it's unused and in its original packaging.

How to return

Bring it to the shop with your receipt, or email us your order number and we'll send you a prepaid label (mainland Portugal only).

Refunds

We refund to the original payment method within 5 working days of receiving the item. We can't refund bikes that have been ridden, but we'll always offer a fair trade-in.
